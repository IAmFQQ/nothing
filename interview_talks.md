下面按面试流程整理了常用英文表达。我按你是面试官来写的。最后一部分是印度同事常用的英文说法，提前了解能减少听不懂的情况。

---

## 1. 开场与寒暄

**打招呼**
- Hi, is this Rahul? I'm Qiang, a software engineer on the XX team. （你好，是 Rahul 吗？我是 XX 组的工程师 Qiang。）
- Thanks for taking the time to join today.
- Can you hear me clearly? / Is my audio okay? （确认音频）
- Could you turn on your camera, please? （远程面试时）

**简单寒暄（30 秒左右就够）**
- How's your day going so far?
- Where are you joining from today? （你现在在哪个城市？）

**说明面试安排**
- Let me quickly walk you through the agenda.
- We have about 60 minutes. I'll start with a brief intro, then we'll talk about your experience, and after that we'll do a coding exercise. We'll leave 5–10 minutes at the end for your questions.
- Feel free to ask me questions at any point.
- Thinking out loud is totally fine. I'm more interested in how you approach the problem.

## 2. 介绍自己和团队

- A little about me: I've been at the company for 4 years, mainly working on backend services.
- Our team is responsible for the search infrastructure.
- We mostly work with Java, Kubernetes and OpenSearch.

## 3. 请候选人自我介绍

- Could you start by briefly introducing yourself?
- Can you give me a quick overview of your background?
- Could you walk me through your resume?

## 4. 深挖项目经历

**问项目**
- Tell me about a project you're most proud of.
- What was your role in that project?
- What was the biggest technical challenge, and how did you solve it?
- Can you draw the architecture / describe the high-level design?
- Why did you choose X over Y?
- What would you do differently if you did it again?

**追问细节（防止候选人只讲 "we"）**
- Which part did you personally work on?
- You mentioned "we". What was your own contribution?
- Can you give me a concrete example?
- What was the impact? Do you have any numbers or metrics?
- How did you measure that?

**行为类问题**
- Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate. How did you handle it?
- Describe a time you had to deal with a production incident.
- How do you prioritize when you have multiple deadlines?

## 5. 技术问答

- Let's switch gears and talk about some technical topics. （转入技术部分）
- What's the difference between a process and a thread?
- How does a HashMap work internally?
- How would you design a rate limiter?
- What happens if this service goes down?
- How would this scale to 10 times the traffic?
- What are the trade-offs here?

## 6. 写代码

**出题**
- Let's move on to the coding part.
- I'll paste the problem into the shared editor. Take a minute to read it.
- Feel free to ask clarifying questions before you start.
- You can use any language you're comfortable with.

**过程中引导**
- Can you walk me through your approach before coding?
- What's the time and space complexity?
- Can you think of a more optimal solution?
- What about edge cases, such as an empty input or duplicates?
- How would you test this?
- Can you run through an example?
- That's a good start. Let's try to optimize it.

**给提示**
- Here's a small hint: think about using a hash map.
- You're on the right track.
- Let's not worry about that for now.

**控制时间**
- We have about 10 minutes left, so let's try to wrap this up.
- In the interest of time, let's move on.
- That's okay. Let's stop here.

## 7. 没听清或听不懂时（非常重要）

印度口音一开始可能不太习惯，直接请对方重复很正常，不算失礼。

- Sorry, could you say that again?
- Could you speak a bit more slowly, please?
- Sorry, the connection broke up a little. Could you repeat the last part?
- Just to make sure I understand, you mean...? （复述确认，最好用的一句）
- Could you type that in the chat? （遇到专有名词时）
- Could you elaborate on that?
- So, in other words, you're saying that...?

## 8. 候选人提问环节

- That's all from my side. Do you have any questions for me?
- Good question. Our team works in two-week sprints...
- I'm not sure about that one. HR / the recruiter would be the best person to answer it. （薪资或福利问题可以这样转给 HR）

## 9. 结束

- Thank you for your time today. It was great talking to you.
- The recruiter will get back to you with the next steps.
- You should hear from us within a week.
- Have a great day! / All the best!

不要当场透露结果，也不要说 "You did great, you'll get the offer" 这类话，用上面的中性表达就好。

## 附：印度英文里的常见说法

| 他们可能说 | 意思 |
|---|---|
| I am having 5 years of experience | I have 5 years of experience |
| Do the needful | 请处理一下 |
| Kindly revert | 请回复（不是"恢复"的意思） |
| Prepone | 提前（postpone 的反义词） |
| My good name is... | My name is... |
| Out of station | 出差或不在本地 |
| I have a doubt | I have a question（不是"怀疑"） |
| Today itself / now only | 强调语气，相当于"就在今天、现在就" |
| Same to you | 回应祝福 |
| Tell me / Yes, tell me | 接电话时的"请讲" |
| Fresher | 应届生 |
| What is your good name? | 请问您贵姓 |
| Passed out in 2018 | 2018 年毕业（不是"晕倒"） |

另外，印度人说话时左右摇头（head wobble）通常表示"嗯、我在听、同意"，不是否定。

**听力上的几个特点：**
- 语速偏快，t 和 d 发音偏卷舌。
- v 和 w 有时不分。
- 有时会把 "th" 读成 "t" 或 "d"，比如 three 听着像 "tree"。

适应几分钟就好了。面试前可以在 YouTube 上搜 "Indian software engineer mock interview"，听 10 分钟找找感觉。
